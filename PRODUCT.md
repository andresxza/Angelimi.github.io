# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Angeli, the recipient of a personal letter from Darwin.

## Product Purpose

A Spanish-language surprise-letter experience that lets Angeli read a personal invitation and revisit memories shared with Darwin.

## Positioning

The letter unfolds through photographs, small interactive scenes, music, and the writer's own words.

## Operating Context

The experience is a static website intended to be opened as a personal link, including on a phone. The existing project is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The page includes a release-date gate and a memory-pairing challenge.
- Its scenes include an envelope, a photo album, tulip photographs, an interactive eye illustration, a planted tulip garden, a scratch-to-reveal date, a typed letter, and a hidden gift.
- Music and interaction sounds use local files. The music can be paused, changed, and adjusted.
- Preserve the Spanish copy, the named people, the current interaction flow, and the relative `img/` and `audio/` asset paths unless Darwin asks to change them.
- No framework, external service, or build step is currently used.

## Evidence on Hand

- Personal letter and scene copy: `index.html`
- Three personal photographs and four tulip photographs: `img/`
- Music and interaction sounds: `audio/`
- No additional testimonials, product claims, or brand guide are provided.

## Product Principles

- Keep the letter and the relationship at the center.
- Let Angeli discover the story through its existing scenes.
- Do not invent personal history or replace the writer's voice.
- Keep the static experience and its supplied media usable on mobile.
